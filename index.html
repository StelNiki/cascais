01 — Get to know the place

A town shaped
by the sea.

Just west of Lisbon, Cascais grew from a fishing village into a welcoming Atlantic town. Its historic centre, open-air cafés, and nearby beaches make it easy to mix a little exploring with a lot of fresh air.

Come for a slow wander through town, follow the coast, or simply find a spot to watch the light change over the water.
